## 1. Confident misses

Every fight gets an out-of-sample prediction (5-fold cross-fitting on 2017–23). A confident miss is a fight where the model gave one fighter at least an 80% chance of being ahead on a card, and all three judges had the other fighter ahead.

- Fights with a unanimous direction across judges: 1302
- Model at least 80% confident: 867 (67% of those fights)
- Confident and wrong against all three judges: **25** (2.9% of confident calls)

**What the model leaned on.** For each fight, the share of the model's case for the favoured fighter that came from grappling (takedowns, control, submissions, reversals) rather than striking:

- Confident misses: 32% grappling
- Confident hits: 33% grappling
- Difference: -2% [95% CI -15%, +12%]
- Favoured fighter also had more total control time: misses 64%, hits 78%

**Tape review sheet:** `reports/tape_review.csv` lists the 25 missed fights, with the two rounds per fight the model was most sure the favoured fighter won (at least one of them must have gone the other way on every card). Suggested categories: *damage not in the stats*, *late-round surge*, *control without offense*, *stat recording error*, *questionable scoring*, *other*.

## 2. Judge styles (anonymized)

Judges with at least 100 cards in 2017–23: 13 (covering 2916 of 4998 cards). Each judge is compared against the shared model fit on all 2017–23 cards, holding the stats fixed, so the comparison is always "same rounds, different judge". Deviations are shrunk toward zero.

- **Striking / grappling weight vs avg:** +20% means this judge's cards respond 20% more strongly to that half of the stat line than the average judge's do.
- **Grappling tilt:** grappling weight relative to striking weight for this judge. Positive = favours grapplers relative to the average judge, separately from how closely the judge tracks the stats overall. When both weights are above zero, the judge's cards simply follow the recorded stats more closely than average (fewer surprises), which is a different thing from a style preference.
- **10-8 strictness:** log-scale shift in the 10-8 threshold. Positive = needs a more dominant round.
- `*` = 95% bootstrap interval excludes zero. `**` = the judge's overall style differs from average after correcting for testing 13 judges (Benjamini–Hochberg q < 0.05).
